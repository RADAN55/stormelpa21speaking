# Storm Speaking Sets — four interactive ELPA21-style speaking tasks (grades 9–12)

One self-contained page, four tabs. Companion to the reading, writing, and listening item-set pages. Students record in the browser, play back, and get a live transcript that feeds a self-check.

| Tab | Task type | Stimulus | Recordings |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Compare Pictures** (`#compare`) | Compare pictures | Two drawn scenes: a science lab and a school library | 1 × 90 s, with a 20 s prep timer |
| **Language Arts** (`#lit`) | Language arts presentation | Nadia's spoken book report on an invented novel, with a book card and a plotted story diagram | 3 × 45 s — detail, analysis, opinion |
| **Visual and Claim** (`#visual`) | Analyze a visual and a claim | Bar chart of how 120 tenth graders prefer to study + Marcus's spoken claim ("most students…") | 2 × 60 s — summarize, evaluate |
| **Observe and Report** (`#observe`) | Observe and report | Animated, narrated five-step demonstration (candle, jar, rising water) with labeled materials; replay or jump to any step | 1 × 90 s |

**Recording:** MediaRecorder in the browser; files stay on the device (IndexedDB) and can be downloaded (`.webm` / `.m4a`) to turn in. **Live transcript:** uses the browser's speech recognition when available (Chrome/Edge); students can edit it, or type what they said where recognition is unavailable. **Self-check:** heuristics for transitions, sensory words, full names, numbers as evidence, time words, materials, and the key events — a guide, never a score.

## Supports
Practice / Test modes · Spanish directions (English audio) · text toggle for the presentation, claim, and narration · glossary per set · sentence stems · text size · dark mode · progress and recordings saved on the device · "Copy my transcripts."

## Teacher key
Add `#key` to the address: classroom rubrics on the ELPA21 point scales (Compare Pictures 0–3, Language Arts Presentation 0–3 per question, Analyze a Visual and a Claim 0–5, Observe and Report 0–5), model responses, self-check criteria, full scripts and narration, picture alt text, and a privacy note.
